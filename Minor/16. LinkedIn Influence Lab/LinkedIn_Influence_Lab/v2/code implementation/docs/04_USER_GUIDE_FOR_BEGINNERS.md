# Beginner's Guide: from zero to your first 6 weeks

No coding needed. You will use the app in your browser.

## Part A - Start the app (10 minutes, once)
1. Install **Python 3.10 or newer** from python.org. On Windows tick "Add python.exe to PATH".
2. Unzip the project. Double-click `run_app.bat` (Windows) or run `./run_app.sh` (Mac/Linux). The first run installs packages, then opens the app in your browser.
3. If it does not open, go to the address shown in the black window (usually http://localhost:8501).

## Part B - Tell the app who you are
1. Open **🧾 Inputs & Onboarding**. Not sure what to write? Press **Load sample inputs** to see an example, then replace it with yours.
2. Choose your path: Professional, Job Seeker or Thought Leader (see **🎭 Persona Lab** if unsure).
3. Fill in role, industry, skills, audience and goal. Add one win, one mistake and one lesson, plus achievements with numbers. These make the posts sound like you.
4. Tick what your LinkedIn profile already has (photo, banner...). Add your LinkedIn URL and Taplio account name. **Never type passwords.**
5. Pick your posting days and the first date. Press **Save inputs**.

## Part C - Optional: connect AI
Open **🔌 Plugins**. Without a key the built-in engine is used. To use ChatGPT choose the OpenAI plugin and paste your API key; for Claude choose the Anthropic plugin. Use **Test the active AI connection**. If anything goes wrong the built-in engine takes over automatically.

## Part D - Let the lab do the heavy lifting
On **🏠 Command Center** press **Run full pipeline**. In a minute you get the audit, pillars, 15+ ideas, drafts, a 6-week calendar, Taplio files, a dashboard, reports and backups.
Tip: tick the demo-data box to preview dashboards now. Demo numbers are fake and labelled DEMO.

## Part E - The human steps (the important ones)
1. **Phase 1:** read the audit, fix the gaps in LinkedIn (photo, banner, headline, About). Copy the optimized headline you like.
2. **Phase 2:** open each draft, replace any `[placeholder]` with something true, edit in your voice, tick **Human reviewed**. Use **Regenerate** or **Rewrite tone (B)** if you want other options. Use **Template C** to turn an article into a post (always keep the source line).
3. **Phase 3:** press **Run Phase 3** again so reviewed posts land in the approved-only Taplio queue. Follow the **Taplio setup steps** tab. Import the `.ics` file for reminders.
4. **Pilot:** publish the first 2-3 posts (marked with a star) and watch what happens.
5. **Phase 4:** after each post, log impressions, likes, comments, shares and profile visits. Read them from LinkedIn's post analytics or Taplio. You need 10+ posts logged for the project to count as complete.
6. **Reply to every comment** within a couple of hours and comment on 5 peers' posts daily.
7. **Phase 5:** run analytics. Read the insights, follow the refined pillar mix for the next cycle, and **Build all deliverables**.
8. **Submit:** your course page accepts a file upload or a Google Drive link. Upload the capstone report from `deliverables/00_capstone_report` (and the other deliverable folders), or share the folder through Drive.

## Part F - Safety net
- Every phase run saves a backup in `backups/`. Restore from **📦 Deliverables & Backups**.
- Re-running Phase 2 first saves your edited drafts in a `before_rerun` backup.
- Something wrong? See `docs/06_TROUBLESHOOTING_FAQ.md` and `logs/lil.log`.

## Glossary
- **Pillar:** one of 3-5 recurring topics you post about.
- **Hook:** the first line; it decides whether people click "see more".
- **Engagement rate:** (likes + comments + shares) divided by impressions.
- **Pilot:** the first 2-3 posts used to test formats.
- **Plugin:** an add-on file for AI, exports or imports.

## Part G - Sample workspace and your own workspace (new in 1.1)
- When you open the app for the first time it shows the **🧪 Sample data** workspace: a fictional person with everything filled in, so you can click through every page and see what a finished project looks like. A coloured bar reminds you that nothing there is yours.
- To use the app for real choose **👤 My workspace** in the sidebar. It starts empty and every page tells you what it needs and in what format.
- In the sample workspace, **🎭 Persona Lab > What changes if I switch** loads the complete sample for another path.
- Want to try the Inputs form? Use **Fill form with sample values**: it only fills the form; nothing is saved until you press Save.

## Part H - Your path decides your tabs (new in 1.1)
Professional sees the **Consistency Hub**, Job Seeker the **Recruiter Hub**, Thought Leader the **Authority Studio**. Each path also logs three weekly KPIs in Phase 4 and sees them against targets in Phase 5. Change path in Inputs or in the Persona Lab.

## Part I - Roles (new in 1.1)
Everyone starts as a **Member**. An **Admin** (passcode set under 🔐 Admin access in the sidebar) can also open the Folder map and the project structure guide.
