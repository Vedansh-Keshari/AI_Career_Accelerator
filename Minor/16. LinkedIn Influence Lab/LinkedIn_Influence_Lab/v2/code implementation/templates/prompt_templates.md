# Prompt templates (Appendix: AI-Guided Workflow)

The app sends these through the active AI plugin and falls back to the built-in engine if none is connected.
You can also paste them into ChatGPT by hand.

## Template A - Content ideation
Generate 5 engaging LinkedIn post ideas about [industry/career topic].
*Tip: add your audience and goal, e.g. "for early-career analysts who want to be noticed by hiring managers".*

## Template B - Tone rewrite
Rewrite this draft in a professional but relatable tone.
*Tip: add "keep every fact unchanged and keep the hashtags".*

## Template C - Article to post
Summarize this article into a LinkedIn post with 3 key takeaways.
*Tip: add "credit the source" and "write in my first-person voice".*

## Extension - Profile audit helper (not in the brief)
Rewrite my LinkedIn About section so it is specific and human, keeps every [placeholder], invents no facts, and ends with a call to action.
